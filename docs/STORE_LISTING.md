## App Name

## Short Description (80 chars max)

## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Dreams fade within minutes of waking. By the time you reach for your phone, unlock it, and open an app, half the dream is already gone.

Remnant does something no other dream journal does: it wakes you up AND captures the dream in the same moment.

Set your alarm. When it goes off, Remnant asks one simple question: "Good morning, what did you dream about last night?" Just talk. No screen to find, no button to press, no typing with bleary eyes. Your voice is captured, transcribed, and saved -- all before your feet hit the floor.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Set your alarm time and your name during a quick one-time setup
2. Remnant wakes you each morning with a gentle spoken prompt
3. Describe your dream out loud -- take as long as you need
4. Your words are transcribed and saved automatically
5. Review, edit, or listen back to your dreams anytime in the journal

COMPANION MODE

Already have a favourite alarm app? Remnant works alongside it. When it detects your other alarm has fired, it skips the alarm tone and simply asks "Are you awake?" Respond with your voice and the dream capture begins -- completely hands-free.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your dreams are yours alone. Every recording and transcription stays on your device. No cloud storage, no accounts, no data collection. Voice transcription happens on-device using Android's built-in speech recognition. We never see your dreams.

FEATURES

- Voice-first dream capture immediately on waking
- On-device speech-to-text transcription (no internet required)
- Standalone alarm or companion mode (works with any alarm app)
- Accordion-style dream diary with monthly organisation
- Audio playback of original recordings
- Selectable voice personalities for the morning prompt
- Smart silence detection -- stops recording when you stop talking
- Fragment detection for short captures with encouraging prompts
- Streak tracking to build your journaling habit
- Weekly recap notifications summarising your dream activity
- Configurable retention for audio and transcripts
- Audio compression for older recordings to save storage
- Dark theme designed for bleary eyes at dawn

BUILT FOR DREAMERS

Whether you are practising lucid dreaming, exploring your subconscious, working through therapy exercises, or just curious about the stories your sleeping mind tells -- Remnant catches what every other app misses.

Every other dream journal waits for you to wake up. We don't.
