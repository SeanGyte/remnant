## Complaints (ranked by frequency)

1. **Data loss / account lockout — the loudest, most emotional theme.**
   Entries wiped by updates (Lucidity), lost on reinstall despite cloud
   backup (Dream Catcher), account lockouts losing 60+ dreams over 4 years
   (Dream Journal Ultimate), no password recovery losing months of grief
   journalling (Duhnnae). Direct pitch for "never leaves the device, no
   account to lose".
2. **Subscription fatigue.** Core features gated behind subs (Oniri,
   DreamNotes $3-5/mo); rare one-off apps explicitly praised ("super worth
   it... not a subscription").
3. **Morning-UX friction** — slow load, multi-tap, bright screens losing
   the dream. Competitors market against it ("one tap... no bright
   screens").
4. **Transcription reliability** (Letterly complaints).
5. **Privacy/cloud unease** — enough vendors now advertise on-device that
   it's a recognised concern.

Praise: voice-first ease (most repeated), one-off pricing, AI art (where
present), habit features.

## Novelty verdict

**No Android app found IS the alarm.** Every Android competitor is a
journal you open after waking (widget/reminder/manual). The alarm-is-the-
recorder loop exists only as iOS-only (SHADOW 2013, MeowAlarm) or
unconfirmed-on-Android (DreamZ). Companion mode (piggyback existing alarm)
and named companion voices: **no competitor at all.** Caveat: do a manual
device-side Play category browse before claiming "only" in listing copy.

## ASO

- Head term: **"dream journal"** (unavoidable). Secondary: "dream diary",
  "voice dream journal".
- **"dream recorder"** — newer, less contested, maps to our mechanic.
- Unclaimed differentiators: "dream alarm", "no subscription dream
  journal", "private dream journal".
- Avoid over-indexing on "lucid dreaming"/"dream interpreter" (different
  intent, contested).
- One incidental datum: App Store "dream interpreter" volume 41 /
  difficulty 2 — verify with a real ASO tool if pursued.

## Pricing

Android journal-only norm: $1.99–$4.99 one-off. Sub tier ($3-5/mo) for AI
apps. iOS outliers to $59-125 lifetime (unverified). One-off pricing is
actively celebrated in reviews. No clean precedent pins Remnant's number —
price as a separate exercise.

## Positioning opportunities (for listing copy)

1. "It's your alarm, not another app to remember to open" — strongest,
   most defensible claim (post device-side verification).
2. Lead with the data-loss fear: "no account to lose, no server to get
   wiped" — answers the market's loudest complaint verbatim.
3. "One-off purchase, no subscription, ever" high in short description.
4. State on-device plainly (market already understands it).
5. Companion voice = headline experiment (unclaimed but unvalidated).
6. "Dream recorder" as secondary ASO anchor.
7. Companion mode kills the switching-cost objection (keep your trusted
   alarm) — no competitor offers it.
